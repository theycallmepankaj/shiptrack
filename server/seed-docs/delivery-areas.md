# Delivery areas and timings

## Cities we serve

ShipTrack currently picks up and delivers in five cities: **Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune and Hyderabad**. You can send parcels within a city or between any two of these cities.

We don’t yet deliver to other towns, PO boxes, or military and restricted areas. Bookings with a pickup or drop outside these cities may be cancelled with a full refund.

## Delivery hours

Drivers deliver between **9:00 AM and 9:00 PM IST**, every day including Sundays. If your estimated delivery time falls outside these hours, the parcel arrives the next morning.

## How long delivery takes

- **Standard**: same-city parcels usually arrive within the same day or the next day. Intercity parcels take 1–2 days.
- **Express**: same-city parcels usually arrive within a few hours. Intercity express parcels usually arrive the next day.

The exact estimate for your parcel is shown when you book and updates live once the parcel is out for delivery.

## Delivery code

When your parcel is out for delivery, a 4-digit delivery code appears on your shipment page. Share it with the driver only when you receive the parcel. The driver needs it to complete the delivery.
