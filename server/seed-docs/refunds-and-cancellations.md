# Refunds and cancellations

## Cancelling a booking

You can cancel a shipment yourself from **My shipments** while its status is **Booked** or **Driver assigned**, which means until the driver picks it up. Once a parcel has been picked up it can no longer be cancelled.

## Cancelled before pickup

If you cancel before pickup, you get a **full refund** of the amount paid. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

## Failed delivery attempts

If the driver can't deliver (for example, nobody is home or the delivery code is not available), the status changes to **Delivery attempt failed** and we try again. If the parcel still can't be delivered, it is returned to the sender. Return shipping is free, but the original delivery charge is not refunded.

## Late deliveries

Every booking shows a promised delivery time. If an express parcel arrives more than 4 hours after the promised time, you can ask for a refund of the express surcharge by raising a support ticket within 7 days of delivery.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If a parcel is lost or arrives damaged, raise a support ticket within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the parcel and packaging. We compensate up to the declared value of the contents, to a maximum of ₹5,000 per parcel. Damage to items that were not packed as described in our packaging guidelines, or fragile items not booked as "fragile", may not be covered.
