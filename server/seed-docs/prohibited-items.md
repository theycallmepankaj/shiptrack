# Prohibited and restricted items

ShipTrack cannot carry some items for safety and legal reasons. If a parcel contains a prohibited item, the driver will refuse the pickup and the booking is cancelled with a full refund.

## Prohibited items

We do not accept:

- Explosives, fireworks, firecrackers, ammunition and weapons, including replicas
- Flammable liquids and gases: petrol, kerosene, lighter fluid, gas cylinders, aerosol cans and paint thinner
- Loose lithium batteries and power banks shipped on their own
- Corrosive or toxic chemicals, acids, bleach in bulk and pesticides
- Illegal drugs, narcotics and prescription medicines without a valid prescription
- Cash, coins, cheque books, gold, silver, jewellery and precious stones
- Live animals, plants with soil, and perishable food that needs refrigeration
- Counterfeit goods and anything prohibited by Indian law

## Restricted items

These are accepted only with care:

- **Electronics with built-in batteries** (phones, laptops) are allowed if the device is switched off and packed in its original box or bubble wrap.
- **Liquids** up to 1 litre are allowed in sealed, leak-proof bottles inside a zip bag. Book them as "fragile".
- **Glass, ceramics and artwork** must be booked as "fragile" and packed as described in our packaging guidelines.
- **Medicines** for personal use are allowed with a copy of the prescription inside the parcel.

## Size and weight limits

Each parcel can weigh up to 50 kg, and no side can be longer than 150 cm. Heavier or larger items need to be split into several parcels.
