# Packaging guidelines

Good packaging keeps your parcel safe and is required for our damage cover.

## General rules

- Use a strong, rigid box that is not damaged. Reused boxes are fine if they are sturdy, but remove or cover old labels.
- Leave about 5 cm of space around the item and fill it with bubble wrap, paper or air pillows so nothing moves when you shake the box.
- Seal every seam with 5 cm wide packing tape, using the H-taping method across the top and bottom.
- Write the tracking ID on the box, or stick the printed label on the largest side.

## Documents

Put documents in a waterproof envelope or a zip bag inside a padded envelope. Book them as "document" for the lowest weight charge.

## Fragile items

- Wrap each item separately in at least two layers of bubble wrap.
- Use double boxing for glass and ceramics: a small box inside a larger one, with cushioning between them.
- Book the shipment as **fragile** so drivers handle it with extra care. Fragile items that are not booked as fragile are not covered for damage.

## Liquids

Liquids must be in sealed, leak-proof bottles, with the cap taped shut, inside a zip bag. Pack them upright and mark the box "This side up".

## Electronics

Switch devices off. Use the original box if you have it, or wrap the device in anti-static bubble wrap with at least 5 cm of cushioning on every side.
