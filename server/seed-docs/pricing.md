# How pricing works

## Price formula

Every price is made of four parts:

- **Base fare**: ₹40 per parcel
- **Distance charge**: ₹1.20 per km of road distance between pickup and drop
- **Weight charge**: ₹15 per kg of chargeable weight
- **Express surcharge**: express delivery costs 1.6 times the standard price (a 60% surcharge)

The total is rounded to the nearest rupee. You always see the full breakdown before you book, and the price you see is the price you pay.

## Chargeable weight

The chargeable weight is the higher of the parcel's actual weight and its volumetric weight, with a minimum of 0.5 kg. Volumetric weight is length × width × height in centimetres, divided by 5000. For example, a 40 × 30 × 20 cm box has a volumetric weight of 4.8 kg, so a light pillow in that box is charged as 4.8 kg.

## Example

A 2 kg parcel sent 10 km by standard delivery costs ₹40 + ₹12 + ₹30 = **₹82**. The same parcel by express costs about **₹131**.

## Payment

You can pay online when booking. Prices include all taxes. There are no extra charges for Sundays, fuel or remote pickup within our service areas.
